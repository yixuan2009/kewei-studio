01概述Overview

「可为工作室」（以下简称"我们"）非常重视你的隐私。本隐私政策适用于我们开发的所有 iOS / iPadOS 应用，包括但不限于 苹智条形码、可为智能记账、3D 打印耗材管理。 "Kewei Studio" ("we") takes your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy applies to all iOS/iPadOS apps we develop, including but not limited to PinZhi Barcode, Kewei Ledger, and FilaTrack.

一句话总结：In one sentence: 我们的应用以"本地优先"为原则，绝大多数数据只保存在你的设备上，我们不会将其上传至任何服务器，也不会用于广告或对外分享。 Our apps are local-first. The vast majority of your data stays on your device, is never uploaded to any server, and is never used for ads or shared with anyone.

02我们收集哪些信息What we collect

2.1 你主动提供的信息2.1 Information you provide

这些信息在你使用应用功能时生成，例如：Information generated when you use app features, for example:

  • 条码 / 二维码的输入内容、扫描结果与历史记录；Barcode/QR input, scan results, and history;
  • 账单条目、账户名称、分类标签与备注；Transactions, account names, categories, and notes;
  • 3D 打印线材的品牌、参数、用量与日志；Filament brand, settings, usage, and logs;
  • 你通过邮件或反馈表单发给我们的内容。Anything you send us via email or feedback forms.

2.2 自动收集的信息2.2 Automatically collected

为了维持应用稳定运行，我们可能在你授权后收集：To keep apps stable, with your consent we may collect:

  • 崩溃日志（不含你的具体业务数据，仅供调试）；Crash logs (no business data, debug only);
  • 匿名的设备型号与系统版本，用于兼容性诊断。Anonymous device model & OS version for compatibility diagnostics.

2.3 我们不收集的信息2.3 What we don't collect

  • 姓名、手机号、身份证件信息；Name, phone number, ID information;
  • 精确地理位置；Precise location;
  • 通讯录、相册全量、麦克风（除应用功能必需）；Contacts, full photo library, microphone (except when required by the feature);
  • 用于广告画像或转售的任何数据。Any data used for ad profiling or resale.

03如何使用信息How we use information

收集到的信息仅用于以下场景：Collected information is used only for:

  • 为你提供应用的核心功能（生成、扫描、记录、统计等）；Providing the app's core functions (generate, scan, record, analyze);
  • 改进应用稳定性、修复故障；Improving stability and fixing bugs;
  • 在你联系客服时核对问题；Verifying your case when you contact support;
  • 法律法规要求我们必须执行的事项。Anything required by applicable law.

04数据存储与安全Storage & security

应用产生的业务数据默认存储于你的设备本地。若你选择启用 iCloud 同步，数据将通过 Apple 提供的 iCloud 服务在你自己的 iCloud 账户内同步，整个过程不经过我们的服务器。 App data is stored locally on your device by default. If you enable iCloud sync, data is synced within your own iCloud account via Apple's iCloud service — never through our servers.

我们采用 Apple 平台提供的标准安全机制（如沙盒、Keychain、文件加密）来保护设备本地数据。但请注意，没有任何技术能 100% 防止数据丢失或泄露，请定期备份。 We rely on Apple's standard security mechanisms (sandboxing, Keychain, file encryption) to protect local data. No technology is 100% loss- or breach-proof — please keep backups.

05第三方服务Third-party services

我们的应用使用以下第三方服务，仅限于必要场景：Our apps use the following third-party services only when necessary:

  • Apple iCloud — 用于你开启同步时数据在本人 iCloud 账户内的同步。Used when you enable sync — within your own iCloud account.
  • Apple StoreKit — 用于内购支付（如适用），交易由 Apple 处理，我们不接触你的支付信息。For in-app purchases (if applicable). Transactions are handled by Apple — we never see payment info.
  • Apple App Store Connect (Analytics) — 用于查看应用聚合的崩溃与下载数据，全部为匿名汇总。For aggregated crash/download metrics — anonymous & aggregated.

我们不集成第三方广告 SDK、不集成第三方数据分析 SDK，不会将你的数据出售或共享给广告网络。 We do not bundle third-party ad SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share your data with ad networks.

06你的权利Your rights

作为用户，你始终拥有以下权利：You always have the right to:

  • 访问：随时在 App 内查看你产生的全部数据；Access: View all your data inside the app at any time;
  • 导出：使用导出功能将数据转为 PDF、Excel、CSV 文件；Export: Export your data to PDF, Excel, or CSV at any time;
  • 删除：在 App 设置中一键清空所有本地数据；Delete: Wipe all local data in one tap from app settings;
  • 申诉：通过邮件向我们提出关于个人信息的任何问题与请求。Appeal: Email us with any questions or requests about your personal information.

07儿童隐私Children's privacy

我们的应用不针对 14 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你是监护人并发现孩子在未经同意的情况下提供了信息，请联系我们删除。 Our apps are not designed for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a guardian and discover that a child has provided info without consent, please contact us to have it removed.
