FEATURES · 功能功能

六大核心功能Six core capabilities

从入库登记到打印日志，覆盖耗材使用的完整生命周期。 From check-in to print logs — the full filament lifecycle.
01

线卷库存Spool Inventory

Spool Inventory

每一卷线材独立档案：品牌、材料、颜色、净重、入手价、购入时间一目了然。 A unique record per spool — brand, material, color, net weight, purchase price & date at a glance.

02

扫码登记Scan to Register

Scan to Register

扫描线材包装上的条码或 NFC 标签，自动填入品牌、材料与参数，省去手动录入。 Scan the packaging barcode or NFC tag — brand, material & settings auto-fill, no manual typing.

03

打印参数Print Settings

Print Settings

为每种材料保存推荐喷嘴温度、热床温度、风扇与速度，下次打印一键复用。 Save nozzle/bed temps, fan & speed per material — reuse them on your next print in one tap.

04

重量追踪Weight Tracking

Weight Tracking

每次打印结束输入用量，自动扣减并预测剩余可打印克数与可完成模型数。 Log usage after each print — auto-deducts remaining grams and estimates how many prints you have left.

05

使用日志Print Log

Print Log

每次打印记录模型、用量、时长与成功/失败标记，复盘打印习惯一目了然。 Each print logs the model, usage, duration, and success/fail flag — review your habits at a glance.

06

低料提醒Low-Stock Alerts

Low-Stock Alerts

设定阈值，当某卷线材剩余克数过低时主动提醒，避免长打印任务半夜断料。 Set a threshold — get notified when a spool runs low so you never run out mid-print again.
