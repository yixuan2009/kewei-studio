FEATURES · 功能功能

六大核心功能Six core capabilities

从一笔记账到月度复盘，覆盖个人理财的完整链路。 From one quick entry to a monthly review — covers your full personal finance flow.
01

多账户管理Multi-Account

Multi-Account

现金、银行卡、支付宝、微信、信用卡分账户管理，余额一目了然。 Cash, bank, Alipay, WeChat Pay, credit cards — manage all balances at a glance.

02

智能分类Smart Categorization

Smart Categorization

根据商家与金额自动推荐分类，越用越准，新建分类支持自定义图标与颜色。 Auto-suggests categories from merchant & amount — improves over time. Custom icons & colors supported.

03

月度报告Monthly Report

Monthly Report

收支趋势、分类占比、同环比变化，清晰的图表帮你看懂消费习惯。 Income/expense trends, category breakdowns, MoM/YoY changes — clear charts on your spending habits.

04

预算提醒Budget Alerts

Budget Alerts

为不同分类设定预算上限，达到 80% 时温和提醒，避免不知不觉超支。 Set budget caps per category — get a friendly nudge at 80% to prevent overspending.

05

数据导出Data Export

Data Export

支持 Excel、CSV、PDF 多格式导出，按月/年/自定义日期范围打包。 Export to Excel, CSV, or PDF — packaged by month, year, or any custom range.

06

本地存储Local-First

Local-First

所有数据默认仅保存在你的设备上，可选 iCloud 同步，账本永远归你所有。 All data stays on your device by default. Optional iCloud sync — your ledger, always yours.
